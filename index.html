1 In short

SehatCare is an offline-first health app published by AIDET LABS, a registered enterprise based in Bengaluru, India. All health information you enter — symptoms, vitals, medicine reminders, family profiles, wellness check-in scores, menstrual cycle logs, diary entries, daily mood check-ins, hydration / sleep / step / meal / workout logs, streak history, and text and voice conversations with the on-device AI Health Assistant — stays on your phone.

4 Network activity — what and why

SehatCare uses the internet only for these operations, all triggered by you:

  1. AI model download. The Gemma 4 language model (E2B or E4B variant, ~2.5–3.6 GB) is downloaded from a source you explicitly pick — HuggingFace (huggingface.co) or Google Drive. No health data is sent with the request. The download server sees only your phone's public IP address, User-Agent, and the fact that a Gemma file was requested.
  2. Government scheme lookup ("From webpage"). If you tap From webpage and provide a URL, SehatCare fetches that URL's HTML for one-time on-device parsing by the AI. The URL is one you typed; only that URL is fetched. Your health data is not sent.
  3. Find Nearby Specialist. If you tap this button, SehatCare opens Google Maps with a search query built from the suggested specialist name. Google Maps then follows its own privacy policy.
  4. Voice AI Health Assistant speech recognition (conditional). When you speak into the Voice AI Health Assistant, SehatCare calls Android's system SpeechRecognizer with the prefer-offline flag set. On phones where an on-device speech recognition engine is installed and active, the transcription happens entirely on your device. On phones without one, Android may fall back to the system's default online recogniser (typically Google's speech service), which means your voice audio is sent to Google for transcription only — this is a system-level fallback SehatCare has no control over. To keep voice fully offline, install an offline speech recognition pack in Android Settings → System → Languages & input → On-device speech recognition. Voice input is optional; the typed AI Health Assistant chat has no such fallback.
SehatCare does not send analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or advertising identifiers anywhere.

Clipboard. If you tap Copy analysis on the AI Insights screen, SehatCare places the analysis text onto your system clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. This action is initiated by you, happens locally on your device, and does not involve any network transmission. Whatever app you then paste into is governed by its own privacy policy.

5 AI processing — where it happens

The AI Health Assistant (Gemma 4) runs entirely on your device after the one-time model download. Every message you type into the chat, every symptom you enter into Symptom Checker, every question fed to the AI for pattern-spotting or briefing generation is processed locally.

On-device vision. The same on-device model also handles image inputs — prescription photos in the Prescription Scanner and meal photos in the Meal & Calorie Log's Photo → estimate calories feature. Photos are decoded, downsampled, and passed to the model locally; the temporary JPEG is written to the app's private cache and deleted after inference. No photo is ever uploaded.

Voice AI Health Assistant. The reasoning half of the Voice AI (Gemma 4) runs on-device just like the typed chat. What sits around it — speech-to-text and text-to-speech — uses Android's system services (SpeechRecognizer and TextToSpeech). Whether those are on-device or route to a cloud provider depends on which engines and language packs your phone has installed. See §4 for details, and §15 for a full breakdown.

SehatCare's own AI never uploads anything. The only voice-related caveat is the Android system speech recognizer described above.

10 Data retention and deletion

Because SehatCare stores everything locally, deleting the app deletes your data. To delete data while keeping the app installed:

  • Individual features have "Clear" / "Delete" controls (Reports, Chat sessions, Medicine reminders, Diary, etc.).
  • To wipe everything the app has stored: Android Settings → Apps → SehatCare → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This resets the PIN, deletes health data, and removes the AI model file.

We have no server-side data, so there is no server-side deletion request to make.

11 Account deletion

SehatCare does not use cloud accounts. There is no account on any AIDET LABS server to delete, because none is ever created.

Your "account" is a local PIN stored only on your phone. To fully remove all traces of your SehatCare data:

  • Option A — uninstall: Long-press the SehatCare icon → Uninstall. All data is deleted immediately.
  • Option B — keep the app, wipe data: Android Settings → Apps → SehatCare → Storage & cache → Clear storage. The app remains installed but with zero data.

Because there is no cloud account, there is no "delete my account" web form. If you have a question about this, email contact@aidetlabs.online.

14 Health data — detailed disclosure

a. What health data SehatCare collects

SehatCare collects the following categories of health-related data, entered manually by you (or, for step count, read from the built-in sensor on your explicit tap):

  • Vitals — blood pressure readings, blood sugar levels, body weight, body temperature, and their timestamps.
  • Symptoms — free-text symptom descriptions you type into the Symptom Checker or Symptom Diary, and the AI's suggested guidance in response.
  • Medicines — medicine names, strengths, dosage instructions, and reminder schedules you create.
  • Medicine label reader output — medicine names and frequencies extracted from photos of prescription labels that you scan.
  • Wellness check-ins — PHQ-9 and GAD-7 aggregate scores, wellness band, timestamp, and a safety flag. Individual questionnaire answers are discarded immediately after scoring and are never stored.
  • Menstrual cycle log — cycle start and end dates, flow level, associated symptoms, and notes you enter.
  • Vaccine record — dates of vaccines you mark as given and any custom vaccine entries you add.
  • Medicine compatibility reference — medicine pairs you look up; the query and result are processed and displayed locally only.
  • AI chat history — the full text of your conversations with the on-device AI Health Assistant, stored locally so you can review past sessions.
  • Daily mood check-ins — a mood value (1–5) and optional note, one entry per calendar day. Used to power the streak counter and the "What you logged" bar chart.
  • Hydration log — number of water glasses logged per entry.
  • Sleep log — hours slept, sleep quality rating (1–5), and optional note.
  • Steps log — daily step count (manually typed, or prefilled from the on-device step counter sensor after your tap) and optional active minutes.
  • Meal & calorie log — meal type, description, and calorie estimate. If you use Photo → estimate calories, the calorie number is derived on-device by the AI from the photo you took; the photo itself is not stored.
  • Workout log — activity name, duration in minutes, and intensity rating (1–5).

c. Processing location

All health data is processed entirely on your device. SehatCare does not transmit any health data to a server, cloud service, or any third party. The AI Health Assistant (Gemma 4) runs locally after a one-time model download — no health data leaves your device during AI inference.

d. Health Connect and Google Fit

SehatCare does not connect to, read from, or write to Android Health Connect or Google Fit. No health data is exchanged with any platform health data store.

Step counter sensor. SehatCare reads the phone's built-in step counter (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) directly through Android's SensorManager, only when you tap "Read from phone sensor" on the Step Tracker. The read is one-shot: we register a listener, receive the current cumulative value, unregister within 5 seconds, and store only today's derived step count in the local Steps log. There is no background service, no foreground service, no periodic polling, and no continuous monitoring. Manual step entry is always available and works without the sensor read.

e. User consent and control

You provide health data voluntarily by using specific features. You may:

  • Skip any feature entirely — the app functions without any health data entered.
  • Delete individual entries within each feature using its built-in Clear / Delete controls.
  • Wipe all health data at once via Android Settings → Apps → SehatCare → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

By entering health data into any feature, you consent to it being stored locally on your device for the purpose described above.

f. No third-party sharing

SehatCare does not share, sell, license, or transmit health data to any third party, advertiser, analytics provider, or AI service. The three network operations described in §4 do not involve any health data.

g. Security of health data

  • Stored in the app's private directory (/data/data/com.sehatcare.offlinehealth/), inaccessible to other apps.
  • ADB backup is disabled (allowBackup="false"), preventing backup to Google servers or a PC.
  • All network activity uses HTTPS; cleartext HTTP is blocked by the network security config.
  • Prescription and meal photos are written only to the app's private cache directory, passed to the on-device AI, and deleted immediately after inference. They are not saved to your gallery, not uploaded, and not retained across app restarts.
  • Clipboard. Text is placed on the system clipboard only when you explicitly tap Copy analysis. Android 12+ shows a small notice each time an app writes to the clipboard, giving you a visible confirmation.

h. Data retention

Health data is retained on your device until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry. Since SehatCare has no server, there is no server-side retention period and no server-side deletion to request.

15 Voice AI & check-in specifics

a. Voice AI Health Assistant

The Voice AI Health Assistant is a hands-free variant of the AI chat, wired to the same on-device Gemma 4 model. What differs is how your words get in and out:

  • Speech-to-text (STT). SehatCare requests recognition from Android's built-in SpeechRecognizer service with the prefer-offline flag (EXTRA_PREFER_OFFLINE = true). If your phone has an on-device speech engine installed for the chosen language, recognition happens locally. If not, Android may transparently fall back to its default online recognizer, which on most phones is Google's speech service — in that case your voice audio is sent to Google purely for transcription. SehatCare cannot override this system-level fallback; it can only request the offline preference. This is disclosed in §4 as a conditional network operation.
  • Text-to-speech (TTS). Replies are read aloud using Android's built-in TextToSpeech engine. Most modern phones ship with on-device voices installed and no network is used. Some devices use cloud voices for higher quality — that behaviour is controlled by your phone's TTS engine settings, not by SehatCare.
  • What SehatCare stores locally: only the recognised transcript text (as part of your AI chat history), and the AI's textual reply. Raw microphone audio is never stored and is not passed through SehatCare's own code — it moves directly between the microphone and Android's system recognizer.
  • To keep voice fully offline: install an on-device speech recognition pack in Android Settings → System → Languages & input → On-device speech recognition. On Pixel and most recent Samsung phones, this is available for English and several Indian languages.
  • Voice input is optional. All Voice AI features are also available via the typed chat, which has no such system-fallback caveat.

b. Daily check-in, streak, and health trackers

  • Daily check-in. Once per day, at a time you can configure (default 09:00), Android's local AlarmManager fires a notification on your device asking how you're feeling. Nothing is transmitted; the alarm is scheduled and delivered entirely locally. If you tap the notification you land on the check-in screen, where a mood tap (and optional note) is saved to on-device storage.
  • Streak counter. The streak number shown on the Home screen is computed on demand by scanning your local logs (diary, vitals, check-ins, and the five lifestyle trackers) for consecutive days with at least one entry. Nothing about your streak or engagement history is transmitted or reported anywhere.
  • Health trackers (Hydration, Sleep, Steps, Meals, Workouts). Each stores only what you type. Steps additionally offers a one-tap sensor read via Android's Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER. Meals additionally offers on-device photo → calorie estimation via the on-device Gemma vision model.
  • Combined AI Insights. The Run AI Analysis button in the Health Trackers hub feeds the last seven days of tracker entries to the local Gemma model and displays a written analysis. No entry is transmitted. Tapping Copy analysis places the resulting text on your system clipboard so you can paste it into any app.

16 Data breach notification

Because SehatCare stores health data only on your device and AIDET LABS operates no server that holds your data, there is no server-side breach that could expose your personal information to us or to third parties.

If a security vulnerability is ever discovered in the SehatCare app itself — for example, a bug that could allow another app on the same device to read SehatCare's data — AIDET LABS will:

  • Publish a security advisory at aidetlabs.online describing the issue, affected versions, and the fix.
  • Push a patched version to Google Play as quickly as reasonably possible.
  • Notify the Data Protection Board of India within the timeframe required by the DPDP Act 2023, where applicable.
  • Notify affected users in-app on next launch of the patched version.

Report a suspected vulnerability privately to contact@aidetlabs.online with the subject line "Security".
